\section{Experiments}
\vspace{-10pt}

Experiments were conducted by defining a hyperparameter search space and letting Optuna to select the configurations that maximized the F1-score across folds. Since the TPE sampler does not guarantee finding the global optimum, we repeated the optimization multiple times to increase the chances of identifying the best configuration (Additional plots in appendix~\ref{app:plots}).[\href{https://github.com/Gradient-Gang/ANN-Challenges}{CODEBASE here}]
