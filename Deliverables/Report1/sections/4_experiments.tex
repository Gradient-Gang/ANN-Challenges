\section{Experiments}

For every trial generated by the Optuna study, the following steps were executed: construction of the encoder (LSTM/GRU, Conv1D, or MultiScaleCNN) and, when applicable, a decoder for autoencoder-based models; application of He initialization to convolutional and linear layers; training over all $K$ folds using PyTorch Lightning; model-level windowing; and aggregation of predictions, when enabled.

Performance is assessed using the macro-averaged F1-score, a metric particularly suitable for class-imbalanced multiclass problems such as pain-level prediction. Additionally, during development we monitored validation loss, per-class precision and recall, training/validation time per fold, and the number of parameters and computational cost of each model.
